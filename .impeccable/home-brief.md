# Home surface brief

## Scope and mode

- Target: `app/page.tsx` and the shared shell it uses.
- Mode: Persuade.
- Audience: prospective students and their responsible adults; current students remain a secondary path.
- Job: understand what INCO offers, feel the institution's identity, and reach the right next step.
- Primary action: explore the academic offer.
- Secondary action: understand the enrollment process.

## Chosen direction

**Future Lab institucional · Optical Lens.** The route is a bright precision object built from mineral white, deep institutional green, brass restraint and documentary photography. The first viewport pairs a monumental "Cuna de campeones" with the software-program photograph; the bottom edge already opens the Banda de Paz chapter.

Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/home-lens.png`.

The generated crest, invented microcopy and exact generated typography are reference-only. Product facts, semantic text, navigation and controls remain real code.

## Memorable moment

The hero behaves like an optical instrument: its photographic lens breathes with pointer and scroll, the program label floats in a separate depth plane, and the next panoramic chapter rises into view without a hard section break.

## Component grammar

- Large surfaces use low-radius, edge-to-edge framing; small controls use full-radius only when ergonomically useful.
- Thin tinted rules replace generic bordered cards.
- Elevation comes from optical blur, overlap and tinted shadow, not floating white rectangles.
- Display type is oversized and tightly tracked; supporting type remains clean and quiet.
- Motion never hides essential content and collapses to a complete static layout under reduced motion.

## Unresolved

- Real enrollment dates, testimonials, institutional statistics and historical dates remain unavailable or unverified and must not be invented.
